Ciao! I am a Marshall H. Stone Visiting Assistant Professor at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. I completed my PhD at the University of Texas at Austin in 2024 under the supervision of Timothy Perutz.
My interests lie mainly in symplectic geometry and in the intersection of algebraic, differential and low dimensional topology. I'm currently focusing on applications of Floer theory to low dimensional problems.

I am a co-organiser of the 5 Colleges Geometry and Topology Seminar, which takes place weekly at UMass Amherst.
